Support Services

You don't have to face this alone. These organisations provide free, confidential support — regardless of your immigration status.

🚨 Emergency & Crisis

Emergency Services

112 or 999

Gardaí, Ambulance, Fire, Coast Guard. Free from any phone.

Samaritans

116 123

24/7 confidential listening. Whatever you're going through, you can talk. Free call.

Pieta House

1800 247 247

Suicide and self-harm crisis helpline. 24/7. Free. Professional counsellors.

Text About It

Text HELLO to 50808

Free 24/7 text service for anyone in crisis. Anonymous.

🧠 Mental Health & Psychiatry

HSE YourMentalHealth Helpline

1800 111 888

National 24/7 mental health helpline. Your GP can also refer you to local mental health services.

SPIRASI

01 838 9664

Specialist support for survivors of torture and trauma. Medico-legal reports, therapy, rehabilitation. Dublin-based.

Jesuit Refugee Service (JRS)

01 814 8644

Psychosocial support, outreach to Direct Provision centres, language support, and integration programmes.

Turning Point

01 872 8880

Counselling and therapy for people from refugee and asylum-seeking backgrounds. Culturally sensitive support.

🛡️ Safety & Victim Support

Doras Migrant Victim Support

083 008 6391

Free, confidential support for migrants who have experienced crime — hate crime, domestic violence, trafficking, labour exploitation. Interpreter support available.

Women's Aid

1800 341 900

24/7 helpline for women experiencing domestic abuse. Free and confidential.

National Rape Crisis Helpline

1800 778 888

24/7 support for survivors of sexual violence. Free and confidential.

Anti-Human Trafficking Unit

1800 25 20 20

Blue Blindfold campaign. Free, confidential, available in multiple languages.

Safe Ireland

090 647 9028

National network of domestic violence services. Find your local service.

LGBT Ireland

1890 929 539

Support and information for LGBTQ+ people in Ireland. Confidential helpline.

💼 Employment & Job-Seeking

New Communities Partnership

01 872 7842

Employment rights support, citizenship applications, social welfare help, education and accommodation support. National network.

Migrant Rights Centre Ireland (MRCI)

01 889 7570

Employment rights, anti-exploitation, advocacy for undocumented workers, and integration support.

Intreo / MyWelfare

After 6 months in the protection process, you may apply for Labour Market Access Permission. Visit your local Intreo centre or gov.ie for forms. Minimum wage: €13.50/hour (as of Jan 2025).

Clare Immigrant Support Centre

065 682 2026

Local Ennis-based support. Outreach to Clare Lodge and other centres. Advocacy, translation, accompanied visits.

📚 Education & Integration

Education & Training Boards (ETBs)

Free English language (ESOL) classes and adult education nationwide. Contact your local ETB.

Nasc Ireland

021 455 4444

Integration support, rights information, and advocacy for migrants in Ireland.

Irish Refugee Council

01 764 7584

Information, advocacy, and integration support. Information hub for protection applicants.

Crosscare Migrant Services

Major national support NGO. Information, advocacy, and practical support for migrants.

📍 Local Support in County Clare

Clare Immigrant Support Centre (CISC)

Unit 6, Clonroad Business Park, Ennis, Co. Clare

Phone: 065 682 2026

Email: ciscennis@gmail.com

Services available in Arabic, French, and English. Dedicated outreach to Clare Lodge and King Thomond DP Centre.

International Protection Office (IPO)

Processes asylum applications. Not an advocacy body — this is the decision-maker.

01 433 2500

ipo.gov.ie

International Protection Appeals Tribunal (IPAT)

If your application is refused, you appeal to IPAT within 15 working days.

protectionappeals.ie

Information for immigrants and protection applicants in Ireland. Built with purpose in County Clare.
