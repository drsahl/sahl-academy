02Services

Support Services

You don't have to face this alone. These organisations provide free, confidential support — regardless of your immigration status.

Last updated: August 2026

Emergency & Crisis

Emergency Services Gardaí, Ambulance, Fire, Coast Guard. Free from any phone. 112 or 999
Samaritans 24/7 confidential listening. Whatever you're going through, you can talk. Free call. 116 123
Pieta House Suicide and self-harm crisis helpline. 24/7. Free. Professional counsellors. 1800 247 247
Text About It Free 24/7 text service for anyone in crisis. Anonymous. Text HELLO to 50808

Mental Health & Psychiatry

HSE YourMentalHealth Helpline National 24/7 mental health helpline. Your GP can also refer you to local mental health services. 1800 111 888
SPIRASI Specialist support for survivors of torture and trauma. Medico-legal reports, therapy, rehabilitation. Dublin-based. 01 838 9664
Jesuit Refugee Service (JRS) Psychosocial support, outreach to Direct Provision centres, language support, and integration programmes. 01 814 8644

Safety & Victim Support

Doras Migrant Victim Support Free, confidential support for migrants who have experienced crime — hate crime, domestic violence, trafficking, labour exploitation. Interpreter support available. 083 008 6391
Women's Aid 24/7 helpline for women experiencing domestic abuse. Free and confidential. 1800 341 900
National Rape Crisis Helpline 24/7 support for survivors of sexual violence. Free and confidential. 1800 778 888
Anti-Human Trafficking Unit Blue Blindfold campaign. Free, confidential, available in multiple languages. 01 795 8280
Safe Ireland National network of domestic violence services. Find your local service. 01 578 3001
LGBT Ireland Support and information for LGBTQ+ people in Ireland. Confidential helpline. 1800 929 539

Employment & Job-Seeking

New Communities Partnership Employment rights support, citizenship applications, social welfare help, education and accommodation support. National network. 01 872 7842
Migrant Rights Centre Ireland (MRCI) Employment rights, anti-exploitation, advocacy for undocumented workers, and integration support. 01 889 7570
Intreo / MyWelfare After 6 months in the protection process, you may apply for Labour Market Access Permission. Visit your local Intreo centre or irishimmigration.ie for forms. Minimum wage: €13.50/hour (as of Jan 2025).
Clare Immigrant Support Centre Local Ennis-based support. Outreach to Clare Lodge and other centres. Advocacy, translation, accompanied visits. 065 682 2026

Education & Integration

Education & Training Boards (ETBs) Free English language (ESOL) classes and adult education nationwide. Contact your local ETB.
Nasc Ireland Integration support, rights information, and advocacy for migrants in Ireland. 021 427 3594
Irish Refugee Council Information, advocacy, and integration support. Information hub for protection applicants. 01 764 5854
Crosscare Migrant Services Major national support NGO. Information, advocacy, and practical support for migrants.

Local Support in County Clare

Clare Immigrant Support Centre (CISC) Unit 6, Clonroad Business Park, Ennis, Co. Clare
Phone: 065 682 2026
Email: ciscennis@gmail.com
Services available in Arabic, French, and English. Dedicated outreach to Clare Lodge and King Thomond DP Centre.
International Protection Office (IPO) Processes asylum applications. Not an advocacy body — this is the decision-maker.
info@ipo.gov.ie · ipo.gov.ie
International Protection Appeals Tribunal (IPAT) If your application is refused, you appeal to IPAT within 15 working days.
protectionappeals.ie
